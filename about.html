Hi, I'm Suzy

Full-time wanderer, part-time storyteller.

I grew up chasing the smell of salt air through the narrow streets of Lisbon, and somewhere between the yellow trams and the miradouros, I fell in love with the idea that the world was bigger than my little corner of Portugal. A one-way ticket and a half-packed backpack later, "Wander With Suzy" was born — equal parts travel journal, love letter, and excuse to eat street food in every country I visit.

These days home base is still Lisbon, but I'm rarely there for long. I write about the places that surprise me, the meals that stop me mid-sentence, and the small, unglamorous moments in between flights that make travel worth it. If you're looking for a friend to plan your next trip with — or just live vicariously through mine — pull up a chair. There's always room for one more at the table.
